## E.V. Solar modifications

The repository Git history records the exact authorship and date of each change.
E.V. Solar-specific work includes, among other changes:

- E.V. Solar branding and user interface changes;
- solar-first Tesla charging behaviour;
- home-battery-aware charging and battery protection rules;
- scheduled / off-peak charging behaviour;
- charging history and energy attribution features;
- solar charging forecasts and forecast-related calibration work;
- Fronius-related integrations and configuration;
- deployment, security and recovery improvements.

This list is descriptive and is not intended to replace the Git history as the
authoritative record of individual changes.
